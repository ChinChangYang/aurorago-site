朗讀解說
到「設定」›「輔助使用」›「閱讀與朗讀」打開「朗讀螢幕」。把它加進控制中心，在複盤畫面點它，或用兩指從螢幕最上方往下滑，就會唸出畫面上的解說。iPad 上兩指下滑若移動了視窗，到「設定」›「多工處理與手勢」選「全螢幕App」，或用控制中心。

Hearing the explanations
Turn on Speak Screen in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak. Add it to Control Center and tap it in the review, or swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen, and the explanation on the screen is read aloud (in Chinese). On iPad, if the swipe moves the window, choose Full Screen Apps in Settings › Multitasking & Gestures, or use Control Center.
Rung choice (自選級位)
A one-time in-app purchase. On another device, or after reinstalling, tap 還原購買 (restore purchases) in the rung panel with the same Apple Account.
