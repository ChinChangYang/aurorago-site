Hearing the explanations
The auto-explanation reads itself aloud as it writes, in a Chinese voice synthesized on your device; it needs an A14 chip or later (iPhone 12, iPad Air 4th generation and up), and downloads its voice data, about 200 MB, the first time. Set its volume in the app’s 設定 › 聲音 › 語音 (0 turns it off). On older devices, use the system’s Speak Screen: turn it on in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak, then swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen in the review, or tap it in Control Center.
Rung choice (自選級位)
A one-time in-app purchase. On another device, or after reinstalling, tap 還原購買 (restore purchases) in the rung panel with the same Apple Account.
