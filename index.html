朗讀解說
到「設定」›「輔助使用」›「閱讀與朗讀」打開「朗讀螢幕」。之後在複盤畫面對 Siri 說「朗讀螢幕」，或用兩指從螢幕最上方往下滑，就會唸出畫面上的解說。iPad 上兩指下滑會移動視窗，把「朗讀螢幕」加進控制中心再點它較方便。

Hearing the explanations
Turn on Speak Screen in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak. Then, in the review, say “Speak Screen” to Siri or swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen, and the explanation on the screen is read aloud (in Chinese). On iPad, where a two-finger swipe moves the window, add Speak Screen to Control Center and tap it there.
Rung choice (自選級位)
A one-time in-app purchase. On another device, or after reinstalling, tap 還原購買 (restore purchases) in the rung panel with the same Apple Account.
